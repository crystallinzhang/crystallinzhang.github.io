## 2024 Public Transit Usage
*22 Apr, 2025*

Here's a map of my Ontario public transit usage in 2024. Each dot is a station at which I tapped on. You can zoom out to see my visits to Waterloo and Guelph.

I downloaded the data through my PrestoCard account. This was basically all vibecoded with Gemini. *(Edit, 07/06/2026: wow LLMs have become much better.)*

I predict my 2025 public transit usage will look fairly similar. I plan to spend time exploring the East end this summer but that will largely be done on foot or bike.
